About us

We are a fellowship ignited by the love of Christ, a spiritual home for youth and international students seeking to grow in faith and community. We gather not just for meetings, but to experience the transforming presence of God together, building a family where every person is known, discipled, and encouraged to walk boldly with Jesus.

Who we are

Passionate about Jesus and people

We are a growing community of believers from different backgrounds, ages and cultures. We believe church should feel like family and every person should find a place to belong.

Our gatherings include worship, Bible teaching, prayer, fellowship and opportunities to serve.

Our values

Love God: “Love the Lord your God with all your heart.” (Matthew 22:37) We pursue Jesus with wholehearted devotion.

Live the Word: “Your word is a lamp to my feet.” (Psalm 119:105) Scripture shapes our beliefs, choices, and lifestyle.

Walk in the Spirit: “Be filled with the Spirit.” (Ephesians 5:18) We depend on the Holy Spirit for power, guidance, and transformation.

Love People: “Love one another as I have loved you.” (John 13:34) We build a Christ-centred family for youth and international students.

Make Disciples: “Go and make disciples of all nations.” (Matthew 28:19) We equip believers to grow, serve, and lead.

Live Holy: “Be holy, for I am holy.” (1 Peter 1:16) We pursue purity, integrity, and Christlike character.

Share the Gospel: “You are the light of the world.” (Matthew 5:14) We boldly carry the hope of Jesus to campuses, cities, and nations.
